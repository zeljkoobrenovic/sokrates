## GitHub Sponsors profile

Use the following short description and profile body in the [Sponsors dashboard](https://github.com/sponsors/zeljkoobrenovic/dashboard). This document stores the copy; changing it does not update the profile automatically.

### Short description

Help keep Sokrates free, independent, and open source. Support development, maintenance, testing, and documentation.

### Profile body

I'm Željko Obrenović, the creator of [Sokrates](https://www.sokrates.dev/).

Sokrates helps developers, software architects, and engineering teams understand their codebases. It analyzes source code and Git history and produces reports about size, structure, duplication, dependencies, contributors, and trends.

**Help keep Sokrates free, independent, and available to everyone.**

Your sponsorship helps me spend time on:

- Improving the analysis, fixing bugs, and preparing releases.
- Testing compatibility across languages and platforms.
- Maintaining documentation and examples that help people use the tool.

If Sokrates helps you better understand your code, please consider supporting its development. Individuals, teams, and companies are welcome to contribute, whether one-time or monthly.

Sponsorships support my open-source work, including Sokrates. Sokrates will remain free and open source under the MIT license, including for commercial use. Sponsorship is entirely voluntary.

Thank you for helping sustain independent open-source software.
